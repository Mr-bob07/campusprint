About CampusPrint

"Your Campus. Your Shop. Your Print."

Who We Are

CampusPrint is an innovative multi-vendor online printing marketplace tailored for university students (commencing in the DIU–Ashulia university zone) and local neighborhood print vendors.

Similar to how Foodpanda connects hungry customers with nearby eateries, CampusPrint bridges university students facing tight deadlines with local printing businesses. Students compare transparent shop-specific pricing, upload their documents remotely, and receive quick delivery or grab self-pickup with zero queue time.

Our Core Principles

  • Student Convenience: No more waiting in long queues before morning classes or thesis submission deadlines.
  • Empowering Local Vendors: Giving small printing shop owners digital tools to manage orders, customer loyalty, and business analytics.
  • Zero Financial Barrier (COD Only): Safe and accessible Cash on Delivery / Cash on Pickup — no credit cards or complex mobile wallets required.
  • Shop Freedom: Every vendor sets their own pricing, delivery areas, and loyalty discount points.
